Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to tow it?

Your vehicle needs a tow package or brake assist/control — a tow/haul button alone doesn't count. Hitch is a 2 5/16" ball. Message us with any questions about your setup before booking.

Is a security deposit required?

Yes — a refundable damage deposit is collected at pickup. $50 is held back if the black and grey water tanks aren't emptied at drop-off.

Can I bring pets?

Pets are welcome with a pet deposit. Please mention this when booking so we can prepare the trailer accordingly.

What are pickup and return times?

Pickup starts at 11 AM and return is by 3 PM. You get 150 free miles per day of your trip.

What's your cancellation policy?

Moderate cancellation policy — reach out to us directly for the specifics on your dates.

Any other rules?

No smoking in or around the trailer, no music festivals, no generator use, and renters must be 21 or older.
